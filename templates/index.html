Frequently asked questions

Is my TikTok data saved?

No, Your TikTok export is processed in memory and is not stored in any databases or intentionally written to any form of persistent storage.
Visualisations are generated in memory. Matplotlib figures are saved onto a BytesIO buffer rather than being saved onto disk.

What data is analysed?

The only fields that are analysed and created into a dataframe for visualisation is Watch History and Login History, unused or sensitive fields are removed before the data is processes and the visualisations created.

Why does processing take a few seconds?

The processing takes a few seconds due to the fact that the amount of data being read, validated, processed, and figures being generated are quite computationally heavy.
Additionally, it was a design decision to remove the unused / sensitive information before being converted into a DataFrame to prevent analytics from being done at all.

What are the limitations of the analytics?

Most of the dashboard's metrics are inferred from timestamps and activity records available on the TikTok export rather than directly measured by TikTok. Basic counts like the videos watched, and logins come directly from the export.
The export doesn't give enough / detailed information about exact watchtime, total screentime, completion rate, topics and tags for each video. The dashboard estimates some of the data displayed and due to this may not be entirely correct.
